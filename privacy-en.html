Privacy Policy

Effective: November 22, 2026

Sumikin does not collect personal information such as your name or email address. What you record stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

1. About this app

Sumikin (the "App") is an app for seeing the estimated recovery of your muscles, developed and run by an individual developer (the "Developer").

2. Information handled

The App does not obtain personal information such as your name, email address or location. No account is needed.

What you record in the App (the parts trained, how hard you went and when, notes that a part is still sore or has recovered, recovery time adjustments, and your settings), and the water, writing and splattered ink on the home screen, are stored only in a database and files on your device, and are not sent to the Developer or anyone else. The App does not send crash reports.

The App does not read or write Health (HealthKit) data. It does not use the camera, the microphone, your photos or your contacts.

3. Notifications

The App shows notifications scheduled on your device for when a part recovers (local notifications). No information is sent anywhere for this. You can change the permission in iOS Settings at any time.

4. Widgets

To display widgets, the App saves the recovery state calculated from your records, and a picture of the water and writing shown behind the body map, in a shared area on your device (an App Group). This never leaves your device.

5. In-app purchase

The purchase of Sumikin Mastery (an in-app purchase) is processed by Apple (App Store). The Developer has no access to your payment details, such as your credit card. The only party the App communicates with to check a purchase is Apple, and the Developer receives nothing that identifies a buyer.

6. Sending data and third parties

  • Your records are not sent to any server. The App works offline.
  • Your information is not provided to third parties.
  • The App uses no analytics or advertising services that track how you use it.
  • The App's only network communication is for the in-app purchase above.

7. Exporting

Choosing "Export records" puts your records and settings, and your writing and water, into one file (JSON) and hands it to the iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes. The file contains your records, so please take care of it. Your Sumikin Mastery purchase is not included in the file.

8. Backups

The App's data is stored on your device and is included in standard iOS device backups (iCloud or a computer). These backups are made under your Apple Account, and the Developer has no access to them. The App itself does not store data in iCloud Drive or on any server.

9. Deleting data

You can delete your records with "Delete all records" in Settings (your settings are kept). The water on the home screen can be cleared with "Start the water anew" under Ink and leaf, and the writing and splattered ink with "Wipe out the writing and splatter". Deleting the App removes all of its data, since it is stored only on your device.

10. Changes

This policy may change with laws or new features. Important changes will be announced on this page.
